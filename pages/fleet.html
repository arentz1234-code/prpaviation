Cirrus SR-22 G6

The world's best-selling high-performance single-engine aircraft for over a decade. The SR-22 combines speed, efficiency, and advanced safety features including the revolutionary Cirrus Airframe Parachute System (CAPS), which has no failures when used within its operating range.

Our SR-22 G6 (N528M) features Garmin Perspective+ avionics, air conditioning, and Flight Into Known Icing (FIKI) capability. Enjoy an intuitive glass cockpit experience with synthetic vision, traffic awareness, and advanced navigation.
