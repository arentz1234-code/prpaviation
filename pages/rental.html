Rental Requirements

What you need to rent from PRP Aviation.

Pilot Certificate

Valid Private Pilot certificate or higher with current medical and flight review.

Experience

Minimum 100 total hours, 50 hours complex/HP, and recent Cirrus experience or checkout.

Checkout Flight

Complete a checkout with our instructor to demonstrate proficiency in the aircraft.

Insurance

Proof of renter's insurance or purchase coverage through our program.

Payment

Valid credit card on file. We accept major credit cards and offer block time discounts.

Documentation

Complete rental agreement and provide copies of licenses and insurance.
